# NEW

### **Forced Surgery** (3 KP)
Robert has studied the vulnerabilities of various anatomies. By taking a -2 circumstance penalty to a strike, he may attempt to target a vital weak point. Make a medicine check against the level based DC of the target, on a success, any precision damage from the sneak attack feature is converted to persistent bleed damage.

### **Debilitating Operation** (2 KP)
_Prerequisite_. Forced Surgery
_Requirements_. Robert is using Forced Surgery on a strike
Robert's deepened understanding of anatomy allows him to perform more targeted procedures. When he critically succeeds on the medicine check for Forced Surgery, he can inflict one of the following effects.
* The target becomes Clumsy 2 until the end of Roberts next turn.
* The target becomes Dazzled for 1 minute. 
* The target becomes Drained 1.
* The target becomes Sickened 1. 

### **Eager Disciple** (4 KP)
_Frequency_. Once per minute. 
_Requirements_. Robert's next action is a strike against a creature that was damaged by his spiritual leader since his last turn.
Robert is guided on his path by a spiritual leader. To qualify as a spiritual leader, a creature must have led Robert in prayer during the last 24 hours. Robert casts True Strike as a free action.

## Arbiter of the Dusk
### **Twilight Insight (2 KP)**
_Passive_
Robert gains a +2 status bonus on Perception checks to _sense motive_ against Evil creatures, enhancing his ability to discern hidden motives or truths.

### **Shadow Pact (1 KP)**
_Frequency: Once per week, costing 1KP per use._  
Robert can forge a magically binding agreement, ensuring honesty and adherence to the terms. Both parties must agree willfully to the terms. If broken or completed, the offending party suffers a consequence that Robert chose at the forging of the pact. 
Consequences:
- Curse the offender with a 1/4 max HP reduction of their total. 
- Immediately deal 40 damage of a damage type of Roberts choosing to the offender.
- The targets location is known to Robert at all times (barring divination magic blocks).
- Remove a sense from the offender.
- The target must reveal a secret agreed upon at the pact's forging.
For counteracting purposes, the effect's level is equal to half of Roberts level at the moment the pact was forged.
