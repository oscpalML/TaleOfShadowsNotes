Affiliated tags for spells:
*Light, Positive, Good, Healing or Fire*
## Lorekeeper

**Esoteric Counter (2 KP)**
*Frequency.* Once per day.
*Reaction*
When a foe Casts a Spell and you can see its manifestations, you can retrieve one of your scrolls in order to attempt to counteract it.  The expended spell scroll must contain a spell of the same school as the triggering spell and not be more than 1 level less than it. If the spell is of an affiliated tag, gain a +1 circumstance bonus on the counteract check.  

**Arcane Archive (3 KP)** *(BOUGHT)*
Nazromir can create a mental personal magical archive where he stores knowledge from the scrolls he uses. Once per day, he can recall and cast a spell from his archive without needing to use a scroll, provided he has used that spell scroll in the past.

**Archivist of the Canon (2 KP)** 
Nazromirs divine heritage is aligned with the domain of Knowledge. He gains the Scholarly Recollection devotion spell.

**Exorcist’s Grasp (2 KP)** (Reserved for quest)
Nazromir has long studied the soul and possessions. He gains the Eject Soul devotion spell. If the target soul is not inside its original body, the spell loses the incapacitation trait and Nazromir can spend 1 KP to reduce the target success by one stage and have the ejection be permanent on a failure. 
## Divine Paragon

**Conduit of Life (2 KP)** *(BOUGHT)*
Blessed be those touched by your presence. You gain the Lay on Hands devotion spell.

**Divine Inspiration (3 KP)**
*Frequency.* Once per hour.
*Trigger*. You critically succeed a Strike or Athletics check.
*Free action*.
Your heroic deed inspires your allies and toughens their resolve. You gain the effect of a 1st-level *bless* spell and allies gain a +2 status bonus to Will saves while in the aura.

**Dawnflower's Guidance (2 KP)**
You serve as a guiding light for aspiring followers of the Dawnflower. You gain an aura which affects allies within 15 ft that have prayed to Sarenrae in the last 24 hours. Affected creatures gain a +1 circumstance bonus to any check related to Sarenrae's core teachings (such as healing, acts of mercy, or striking down undead). Additionally, an affected creature does not take a circumstance penalty from making a non-lethal attack with a lethal weapon.

**Divine Gaze (3 KP)**
*Free action.*
*Trigger*: you attempt a Perception check.
*Frequency.* Once per hour.
*Prerequisite.* Nazromir has his divine eye uncovered.
You gain a +2 status bonus to the Perception check.

**Herald of the Sun (2 KP)**
*Free action.*
*Frequency.* Once per day.
Your eye's divine glow intensifies, granting you a semblance of the aura and authority of a herald of Anor. For 10 minutes, you gain a +2 status bonus to Diplomacy, Intimidation, and Deception checks against anyone who respects or fears divine power.
## Slayer

**Study Defences (2 KP)**
You can forgo the normal effects of a Recall Knowledge check regarding a creature you can see to instead attempt to understand its movements and physiology to find an opening in its defences. The regular Recall Knowledge effects are replaced by the following:
*Critical Success*. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your attack rolls against the target until the end of your turn.
*Success*. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on your next attack roll against the target before the end of your turn.
*Critical Failure*. You miscalculate, instead leaving yourself open for attacks. You are flat-footed until the beginning of your next turn.

**Cursed Effigy (3 KP)**
You gain the Cursed Effigy thaumaturge class feat.

**Methodic Fighter (3 KP)**
*Flourish.*
*Frequency.* Three times per day.
*Requirements.* Exploit Vulnerability.
1 action.
You use your strikes to set up carefully calculated manoeuvres. Make a Strike, if the attack succeeds, you may attempt to Trip, Grab or Shove the same target. Only increase your multiple attack penalty once and after using this feature. 

**Iron Fist of Durgan (3 KP)** 
*Prerequisite.* You are wearing the armbands of Durgan Thorason.
Your clasp is that of the mountain itself. Creatures that are grabbed by you suffer the following effects while grabbed:
* The DC of flat checks to attempt manipulate actions is increased to 11.
* The grabbed creature takes a -2 circumstance penalty to Escape attempts.
* The armbands of Durgan Thorason ignore any language penalties to the Demoralize action against the target. Additionally, when the target would decrease its frightened condition, it does not if the condition was caused by the armbands.
