Upon arriving back in [[Ghostlight]], the party heads for the lighthouse. They tell [[Captain Marrowveil]] about their success and show him his old compass that they recovered. Upon being handled the compass, [[Captain Marrowveil]] suddenly fades away, having passed to the other side.

The party once more tries to help [[Thornwick]], but find no luck, even after attempting to be completely silent. Upon making their way back to the harbor, the party starts to notice strange signs. Puddles which reflect incorrectly, animals moving in weird geometric patterns. After a while of frantically trying to find the source of the phenomena, it is made clear as a ghost pirate ship descends upon the city. A landing crew of ethereal pirates disembarks and starts making their way to the lighthouse. [[Thornwick]] addresses the group, holding the line outside the lighthouse. The group, presenting themselves as [[The Drowned Court]], and demand that [[Thornwick]] reveal to them the information they seek. [[The Drowned Court]] give [[Thornwick]] four days to give in to their demands. 

Afterwards, the party and [[Thornwick]] decide to ally themselves do defend against [[The Drowned Court]]. [[Thornwick]] teleports the party to a remote volcanic chamber, located bellow the [[Achaekek Temple]]. The first chamber contains three piedestals, two are empty whilst the third carries a hammer. One of the empty piedestals kept something like a cup, with a circular recess. The other empty piedestal has two metal forks which seems to have held something like a weapon. The two missing items where stolen roughly 150-200 years ago, [[Thornwick]] is trying to destroy the hammer, as none of the items should exist according to his mind. 

The party is then lead to a second chamber containing murals. All murals contain depict five heroes, [[The Watchers]]. The heroes consist of figures which roughly resemble a female paladin, a ranger, a male paladin, a sorcerer and some kind of weird pirate. 

>The first mural depicts a fire giant meeting the five heroes on a volcano. The mural is partially blown away by [[Thornwick]]'s magic blasts. In the background a small serpent is slithering around the volcano.

>The second mural shows the five heroes and the giant slaying a dragon. In the background the same serpent has grown larger. The head of the serpent is blasted off by magic. 

>The third mural illustrates a battle with the same serpent. Now the serpent is even larger  and more detailed and is moving through flames.

> The fourth mural is almost entirely blown away.

> The fifth mural is also largely blown away, one can make out the feet of the five heroes and guess that it is some sort of heroic scene.

> The sixth and final mural depicts two armies against each other, a central figure has been blown away. One army is lead by the five heroes. The mural transitions into only the five heroes remaining, the armies gone. 
	"Marrowveil blir chockerad/blir tvungen hålla tungan rätt i mun när naz frågar om det handlar om mid-sea isles meteoren." fråga malte om förtydligande

The party is then lead to a final room with only a single mural. This mural depicts the five heroes sacrificing something of themselves to a dark void in exchange for information.
![[void_mural.jpeg|700]]
[[Thornwick]] shares that the paladin is [[The Watchers#Omen|Omen]] and that he retained his heart when all the other heroes sacrificed theirs. It is assumed that he betrayed the other heroes or betrayed the entity they sacrificed to. Someone is searching for whatever [[The Watchers#Omen|Omen]] stole. 

[[Thornwick]] speaks about the world before, the one that was remade by [[The Watchers]]. He exists to safeguard the knowledge of and from that world since it should not exist anymore.

[[Thornwick]] discovers some runes in one of the murals. [[Nazromir]] copies the runes and deduces that the runes are strikingly similar to the earliest giant scripts. The same runes cover the hammer that is on the third piedestal.  

[[Thornwick]] shares that [[The Drowned Court]] have long laid claim to [[Ghostlight]]. Now with [[Captain Marrowveil]] gone they likely see their opening. They are likely tied to [[The Unspeakable|The Unspeakable/The Oracle]]. They possess magic which should not be. 

[[Ashenfell]] is somehow uniquely tied to the world that was before.

[[The Unspeakable/The Oracle]] seems to be collecting the characteristics of mortals, trying to complete itself. It is gathering empathy, heritage, hope and so on.

After a gruesome ritual, the party arrive back in [[Ghostlight]]. They decide to ambush [[The Drowned Court]] early. Thornwick gives [[Nazromir]] a scroll of Cone of Cold. Meanwhile, Berit of [[Berit´s Bobs and Baubles]] investigates and refines the occult pendant that [[Nazromir]] found amongst [[Mayor Edwin]]'s belongings.  

[[Thornwick]] helps cast a Phantom Ship ritual and the party leaves to ambush [[The Drowned Court]]. They attack the leader [[K'Tharn]] and slay his brute enforcer. Upon dying [[K'Tharn]] is sucked into a strange portal, along with [[Robert Sköld]] who was mind-controlled into jumping in. 

The party daringly jump in to the portal in search of [[Robert Sköld]]. Inside they are met by a bleached and barren landscape. [[The Unspeakable/The Oracle]] fills their minds with honeyed words and tempting offers, trying to pray upon their insecurities and pain. The party resists the visions and return back through the portal, destroying it from the other side. [[Nazromir]] screams out in rage, troubled by the visions and temptations presented.

The party find prisoners of [[The Drowned Court]], rescues them, and takes them back to Ghostlight on the 29th. (Canon skottår?)
