On the way back to [[Blighthollow]], the party suddenly notices a parting of the cloud in the distance. The rays from the parting are cast upon a singular ship. Upon approaching the party notes the name of the ship, [[The Prometheus]]. Upon [[The Prometheus]] the party finds the crew incinerated to death, seemingly having been scorched by some divine wrath. The ship carries many barrels, some labelled [[Aurora Trading Corporation]], a company famous for trade in luxury goods. 

[[Robert Sköld]] hears a unsettling sound from below deck, that of a neck being snapped. The party quickly scrambles to prepare themself for whatever would ensue. They proclaim their presence and shout for whatever lurks below deck to announce itself. Coming up the stairs, they are met by a elven figure clad in white robes. His muscles are well defined and he is aglow with divinity. Nazromir reveals his glowing eye and the figure takes a sharp interest to him. He tells Nazromir to follow the charted course of the ship in order to find his true origins. He also shares that the daughter of [[Goliander Starchild]], ??, has been kidnapped by [[Besmara]] and that he intends to take over that custody. 

The figure flies away up into the clouds. Nazromir considers if perhaps it could have been the herald of [[Iomedae]], at least he excludes all other heralds. In studying the charted course in navigation room, the party sees that the ship departed [[Iomedar]], headed for [[Lumin]], then [[Bane's Wharf]], then [[Duskhaven]] and finally the [[Mid-sea islands, Kailani]]. The shipping manifests indicate a cover mission of delivering supplies for the crusade. The records indicate that the mission has procured a large quantity of [[Midnight Dew]] and is now headed for the final destination.

 >Ship manifest to [[Loháven]] states this is a Crusade logistic vessel, carrying tents and tools from [[Iomedar]] to [[Shallowport]]. Ship manifest to [[Celebros, The Silver Coast]] states that the vessel landed in [[Lumin]] and is on its way to haul food to feed the people of [[Bane's Wharf]].

The party also locates a magical flare gun.

Below deck, the party finds the corpse of a gaunt sickly elven man. The man has been tortured and life has just passed from his body. He has arcane tools and markings covering and piercing his arms, not too dissimilar from those employed by [[The Brightfangs]]. They are painful and drastic arcane enhancements. The man is a fetchling, just like [[Robert Sköld]]. [[Lucian]] casts a spell to be able to converse with the corpse, it is revealed that he was an enemy of [[Ci’taon]]. Nazromir extracts and brings the tools installed into his arms.

After casting a alignment detection spell, Nazromir notices a enormously evil aura emanating from [[Bob the Balchemist|Bob's]] equipment. He tasks [[Robert Sköld]] with retrieving whatever it is. Upon extracting it, Nazromir and Robert recognises it as [[The Cup of Acheakek]] which they encountered in the [[Achaekek Temple]]. After Nazromir removed the curse from [[Bob the Balchemist]] previously, he apparently instantly grabbed it again and kept it secret from the party. The cup has been twisting his alignment and trying to get him to murder people this whole time. The problem then arises however, of Robert now also being cursed by the cup, he fails to resist it and tries to sacrifice his own blood to the cup. Nazromir seeks the help of [[Lucian]], who is able to draw the curse into himself temporarily. They restrain Lucian so that he will not be able to do any arm whilst under the influence of the curse, however from thin air his curse manifests a knife made out of shadow and cuts the rope restraining him. A clusterfuck ensues which results in Nazromir having to knock out [[Lucian]] and [[Bob the Balchemist]] first, then helping [[Robert Sköld]] knock himself out before the curse transfers back to him from [[Lucian]]. With everyone knocked out, [[Nazromir]] removes the curse from [[Robert Sköld]] and then wakes him.

When [[Lucian]] awakes he feels severely emasculated, feeling puny from the fact that [[Nazromir]] managed to fell him with a single strike. He tries to abandon the party right away but [[Nazromir]] convinces him to cool down and first lift the curse on [[Bob the Balchemist]], after doing so he escapes into the cargo hold of the ship. Feeling guilty, [[Bob the Balchemist]] shares that his real name is [[Brohadil Granitkäke]], crown prince of [[Behl’Gurn]]. He is on a journey to accrue one million gold in a bid to prove his worthiness of claiming the title of crown prince. He is not allowed to use his influence or fame to do so, and thus took up the persona of [[Bob the Balchemist]]. He has many children with his wife, the princess-by-birth Cradena.

Lucian is gone the next morning, leaving only a note behind. The note reads that he must find his own strength and identity, something which he believes he must do himself without relying on others. Nazromir sends him a dream message proclaiming his support for his decision and journey.

The next day, the 2nd of March, [[The Emerald Marauder]] and [[The Prometheus]] arrive in the center of [[Mid-sea islands, Kailani]]. After firing the flare gun the party is greeted by [[Robert Lance]], a smaller boy of roughly 17 years. He mentions [[Kaelen]], the body laying below deck, and a [[Kassara]]. The party deceives Robert and makes him lead them to his higher ups. The party are led to a giant temple in a jungle clearing. The architecture is that of classic [[Lohaven]], like a greek temple. The temple is meticulously kept for, with statues outside.

The party meets two elves, [[Zephyros]] and [[Zylvaris]]. [[Zephyros]] has white exaggerated hair and glows with a blue aura, his movements are temporally inconsistent. [[Zylvaris]] is a shorter woman with crystals growing on her skin, wielding a bow. They shout for [[Elowen]]. They all wear white clothes with gold accents. 

The pair follows the party back to the ship, striking up a conversation with [[Nazromir]]. It is revealed that [[Goliander Starchild]] and [[Kaelen]] are not perfectly aligned, rather they have their disputes. They are revealed to also be infused with divinity, like Nazromir. [[Robert Sköld]] disguises himself as [[Kaelen]] and acts angry. It is clear that the pair fears him, they instinctually reach for their collars when he acts up. 

After arriving back at the temple, [[Robert Sköld]] discovers the key to [[Kaelen]]'s laboratory hidden in [[Kaelen]]'s coat. Inside he and [[Nazromir]] find [[The Soul Forge]], along with many arcane instruments and all of [[Kaelen]]'s notes. [[Zylvaris]] comes by to deliver [[Kaelen]] his tea, upon tasting it, [[Robert Sköld]] detects sleep inducing agents, indicating that the party may not have that much time before their luck runs out. They manage to deduce that [[Kaelen]] is working on something like dampening the soul and controlling divine energy. His notes indicate that he was stuck for many years before finally making breakthroughs after reaching out to [[The Gifted One]]. They find some notes reading:

Nazromir studies the arcane designs of [[The Soul Forge]], and contemplates how to destroy it. The machine is not active right now, but if all control dials were to be fully turned up along with the main power, he reckons it should tear itself to pieces. Fearing that they may not have much more time, Nazromir leaves his mage hand by the main power dial, planning to blow it if things escalate.

Outside they find [[Zephyros]]. Wanting to free his enslaved kin, [[Nazromir]] uses the tools of [[Kaelen]] to deactivate the soul-dampening collar. [[Zephyros]] instantly explodes in a burst of electricity, throwing the party back. In the shock, fearing that their luck has just run out, Nazromir gets his mage hand to pull the dial on the control panel of [[The Soul Forge]]. The other inhabitants of the temple complex descend upon the situation and a fight ensues. [[Bonk]], who was thrown from his flask in the explosion, runs up and tears [[Elowen]] in two before the party is able to get him back in his flask. Just as the chaos reaches its pinnacle, [[The Soul Forge]] explodes. The soul-touching energies emanate and shunts the souls out of all the inhabitants of the temple complex. 

[[Bob the Balchemist]] is also shunted out of his body after he approached the still active [[The Soul Forge|Soul Forge]], but the party are able to coax his soul back. [[Nazromir]] leads [[Robert Sköld]] in a communion and prayer to [[Sarenrae]]. [[Nazromir]] receives guidance about his current predicament whilst [[Robert Sköld]] is tasked to redeem and save [[Lucian]] from a dark storm coming with [[Udún]]'s shadow from the west. 

[[Nazromir]] takes of the collar reading 'B-2' from [[Elowen]]'s body and then performs a resurrection ritual, bringing [[Elowen]] back to life. She quickly starts to be overtaken by blisters, the collar is quickly placed back and the blisters recede. She is revealed to be heavily indoctrinated, believing that [[Kaelen]] only wanted what was best for them and protected them. [[Nazromir]] casts a Word of Truth spell, sharing what he knows to be true, but still she struggles to accept her new reality. She also reveals that the traitor mentioned in [[Kaelen]]'s notes is [[Ci’taon]].
